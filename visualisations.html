Reading “The Gift of the Magi” with an LLM: how language turns loss into value

Hover over any element for the phrases behind it. Use the toolbar at the top right of each chart to zoom or save a PNG.
Research question. How does the story use the symbolism of time, memory and emotional experiences to explore the meaning and value of human life?
Claim tested. The story initially presents time as a mechanical and controlled process through Alistair's workshop and clocks. However, through the Anamnesis machine, memory and emotional experiences become central to understanding human existence. By the ending, the story suggests that a meaningful life is not defined by perfection but by accepting both sorrow and happiness as parts of a complete human experience.

Method note

Phrases were extracted by Claude (Anthropic), claude.ai chat from a prompt written by the researcher, verified word for word against the text, and matched by whole words, longest phrase first, without counting any stretch of text twice. Tests are descriptive: hits within a passage are not independent.
