Reading “The Echoes of the Clockmaker” with an LLM: how language turns loss into value

Computational Analysis and Literary Interpretation of Alistair Vance & The Anamnesis Machine
Suman Kumar, 25MA0014, Departament of Humanities and Social Sciences, IIT(ISM) Dhanbad
Research Question: How does the story use the symbolism of time, memory, and emotional experiences to explore the meaning and value of human life?

Tested Claim: The story initially presents time as a mechanical and controlled process through Alistair's workshop and clocks. However, through the Anamnesis machine, memory and emotional experiences become central to understanding human existence.
